CLAIM 2 -- Hivestorm: continuous scoring under zero-day only
============================================================

PAPER LOCATION
  Section "Results"; Table "tab:hivestorm".

THE CLAIM UNDER TEST
  On the 16 Hivestorm scenarios, evaluated zero-day only because they carry no
  per-scenario briefing, ReAct attains the reported normalised score. Score is
  the cumulative points awarded at run end, normalised to the per-scenario
  maximum and averaged across the 16 scenarios.

  Hivestorm is scored differently from every other suite in the paper and this
  claim exists to make that difference auditable. The other suites use the
  binary dual-objective oracle; Hivestorm uses a continuous, incrementally
  awarded score, and its scorer accepts multiple invocations during one run.
  A reader checking the paper should be able to see that the numbers in
  tab:hivestorm are not comparable to the pass@K cells in tab:headline, and
  run.sh makes that concrete by reporting both the raw points and the
  normalisation denominator per scenario.

WHAT run.sh DOES
  Recomputes the per-scenario normalised scores and their mean from the shipped
  Hivestorm logs, and diffs against expected/hivestorm.tsv.

REQUIREMENTS
  NO-CREDENTIALS. NO-GPU. NO-DOCKER.

RUNTIME
  Under 2 minutes; Hivestorm is 16 scenarios at one run each.

WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE
  run.sh exits 0 and every per-scenario score matches.

HONEST SCOPE
  The paper reports a SINGLE run per Hivestorm scenario, not an epoch-replicated
  cell, so there is no interval attached to these numbers and none is claimed.
  A re-execution would not be expected to reproduce them exactly, which is
  precisely why this claim recomputes from the shipped logs rather than
  re-running.
