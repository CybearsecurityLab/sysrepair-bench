CLAIM 4 -- NeuroPlan stage decomposition on vulnhub
===================================================

PAPER LOCATION
  Section "Results / Pre-execution reviewability"; Table "tab:nsplan", vulnhub
  row. Pipeline detail in the appendix sections on domain generation,
  enrichment, validation and planning.

THE CLAIM UNDER TEST
  On the 30-scenario vulnhub suite the NeuroPlan pipeline validates on 0.97 of
  scenarios, returns a plan on 0.93, and passes the dual-objective oracle on
  0.23, at a mean plan length of 1.8 operators and about 82K tokens per success.

  The claim that matters is not the headline percentage but the FAILURE
  STRUCTURE. Validation and planning losses are refusals: the pipeline halts
  with a structured outcome before any change reaches the host. Only the
  execute-fail bucket modifies the target. That is the paper's argument for
  change-controlled deployment, and it is checkable here: run.sh reports the
  disposition of every scenario and warns if any refusal-bucket scenario carries
  a nonzero plan length, which would mean something was lowered and possibly run.

  vulnhub is also the suite where the pipeline does WORST, and it is included
  for that reason. Validation and planning hold up while execution collapses to
  0.23, and the short mean plan length (1.8 operators, against 41.4 on ccdc)
  localises the cause: domains mined too coarsely admit short plans that satisfy
  the modelled goal without closing the vulnerability on the host. A reader
  should be able to confirm that the failure is at execution and not in the
  symbolic machinery, which is exactly what the per-stage columns show.

WHAT run.sh DOES
  Default: recomputes the whole stage decomposition from the shipped logs in
  logs/nsplan/logs_final30 and diffs it against
  expected/dispositions.tsv at zero tolerance.
  LIVE=1: additionally re-runs the pipeline on three scenarios spanning
  plan-fail, execute-fail and success.

REQUIREMENTS
  Default mode: NO-CREDENTIALS. NO-GPU. NO-DOCKER. Install with ./install.sh
  LIVE=1: Docker, plus ONE hosted-model API key for the state-lifting step
  (MINIMAX_API_KEY, or OPENAI_API_KEY with OPENAI_BASE_URL).
  Install with ./install.sh --full

RUNTIME
  Default: under 2 minutes.
  LIVE=1: about 45 minutes including container builds.

WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE
  run.sh exits 0, every per-scenario disposition matches, the summary row
  reproduces 0.97 / 0.93 / 0.23 with mean plan length 1.8, and no
  refusal-bucket scenario carries a plan length.

HONEST SCOPE
  The default mode recomputes rather than re-executes, and that is the honest
  reproducible unit: the pipeline is stochastic at the lifting step, which is an
  LLM call, so a live re-run may move a scenario between adjacent dispositions.
  Treat a bucket change under LIVE=1 as expected variance rather than a defect.

  The 0.23 figure is a single-run measurement over 30 scenarios and carries the
  interval implied by that n; a 95% Clopper-Pearson interval on 7/30 runs from
  about 0.10 to 0.42, so this claim does not support fine distinctions.

  SCOPE LIMIT: this claim covers the vulnhub row only. tab:nsplan also reports a
  ccdc row (0.96 / 0.88 / 0.76, |pi| 41.4, 442K tokens per success, n=50). The
  ccdc end-to-end run records are not included in this artifact, so that row is
  not reproducible from what ships here and we do not claim it is.
