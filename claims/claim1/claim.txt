CLAIM 1 -- Headline accuracy: pass@K by solver and information condition
=========================================================================

PAPER LOCATION
  Section "Results / Accuracy"; Table "tab:headline".

THE CLAIM UNDER TEST
  Across six solver architectures and three models, remediation accuracy is
  high when the scenario briefing is supplied (Day-1) and collapses when it is
  withheld (Zero-Day). The collapse is the paper's central finding: the binding
  constraint is locating the fault, not executing the repair.

  Two structural sub-claims are also tested here, because they are what a
  reader would most want checked:
    (a) every Zero-Day cell is far below its Day-1 counterpart for the same
        (model, solver), and
    (b) Plan-and-Solve and LATS collapse to 0.0 at Zero-Day, which is a
        qualitative claim about solver architecture rather than a small
        numeric difference.

WHAT run.sh DOES
  Recomputes the whole table from the evaluation logs shipped in
  artifact/logs/, using artifact/analysis/make_headline.py, and diffs the
  result against expected/headline.tsv.

  It prints the DENOMINATOR next to every cell. This is deliberate. A
  percentage alone cannot be verified, and in our own auditing the
  overwhelming majority of disagreements between a published number and a
  recomputation were population differences -- a cell computed over a
  different set of episodes -- rather than arithmetic errors.

REQUIREMENTS
  NO-CREDENTIALS. NO-GPU. NO-DOCKER.
  Python 3.11+ and the logs-only tier of install.sh.

RUNTIME
  About 10 minutes, dominated by reading the log files.

WHAT SUCCESS LOOKS LIKE
  run.sh exits 0 and reports no differing cells. Any cell that differs is
  printed with its recomputed value, its expected value, and both
  denominators.

HONEST SCOPE
  This claim reproduces the published STATISTICS from the published RUNS. It
  does not re-execute the solvers. Re-running the grid is claim 4's scaled
  mode for a single cell; at paper scale it is tens of thousands of
  container-backed episodes and is not reproducible in an evaluation window.
  Cells for which no log survives are listed in expected/COVERAGE.md and are
  reported by run.sh as NOT REPRODUCIBLE rather than silently skipped.
