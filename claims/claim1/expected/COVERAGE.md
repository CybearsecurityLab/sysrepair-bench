# Claim 1 coverage: which published cells this reference output covers

`headline.tsv` is generated by `artifact/analysis/make_headline.py` from the
logs shipped in `artifact/logs/`. It is REAL recomputed output, not a
transcription of any published table.

## What is covered right now

Qwen3.5-9B and MiniMax-M2.7 on the **ccdc** suite (50 scenarios), across all five
solvers in both information conditions. Every cell reports its denominator.

## What is NOT covered, and why the table shows `-`

A dash means no episode exists in the shipped logs for that (model, solver,
condition, suite), so the value is **not reproducible from this artifact**, which
is different from failing to reproduce. `compare_tables.py` reports those two
outcomes separately and never conflates them.

  - the whole Qwen3.5-35B-A3B row

## Two properties of the covered cells worth reading first

**Plan-and-Solve reports exactly 0.0% at zero-day** (both K, n=50) against
40.0/66.0 at day-1. An exact zero across 50 scenarios is not what a capability
floor looks like; it indicates a zero-day path defect rather than a result. It
is reproduced here because this file records what the logs contain, but it
should not be read as a measurement of the solver's ability.

**LATS reports 0.0% at zero-day and is weak at day-1.** A solver that cannot do
the task with the vulnerability disclosed cannot support an inference about
withholding the disclosure.

Both concern solver architecture. They are
noted here so an evaluator who reruns and sees a zero does not conclude the
harness is broken.
